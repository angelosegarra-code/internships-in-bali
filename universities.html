University & Academic Partnerships

Structured placements and documentation support for supervised or credit-bearing internships in Bali and across Indonesia.

Download One-Page PDF Request Information

Academic alignment

We support universities seeking reliable internship placements in Indonesia. Programs can be aligned with supervision requirements, academic calendars, and credit-bearing formats.

Program structure & supervision

Participants receive placement matching, a clear program timeline, local coordination, and check-ins during the internship period. Where required, we can provide coordination support for supervision and reporting.

Documentation available

  • Program overview and schedule
  • Placement confirmation letter
  • Learning-outcome summary (available on request)
  • Local supervisor or mentor details (where applicable)
  • End-of-program confirmation (participation / dates)

Locations & placement fields

Programs are offered in Bali and, based on field availability, in Lombok, Yogyakarta, and Jakarta. Placement fields may include NGOs and community projects, education support, hospitality, creative industries, media, sustainability, and social impact.
