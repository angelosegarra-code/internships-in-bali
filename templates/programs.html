Choose a Location

Start with Bali. If you want something more specific — community projects, sustainability, education support, or a different city pace — choose one of the other locations.

Bali (Flagship)

Our main hub — best for first-time Indonesia interns and anyone looking for a balanced mix of structure, culture, and support.

  • Creative industries (design, media, content, arts)
  • Hospitality and tourism-related roles
  • NGOs and social impact placements
  • Wellness-adjacent roles (community, studios, outreach)

Lombok

A slower pace with strong community and nature orientation — ideal for sustainability, local initiatives, and education support.

  • Community projects and local initiatives
  • Sustainability and environment-related work
  • Education support and youth programs

Yogyakarta

A cultural and academic center — ideal for students interested in arts, heritage, language, education, and local creative ecosystems.

  • Arts and culture projects
  • Education and academic-adjacent placements
  • Local creative industries and community work

Jakarta

Indonesia’s largest city — best for corporate exposure, startups, professional services, and big-city experience.

  • Startups and business operations
  • Corporate and professional environments
  • Media, marketing, and communications

Program Length

  • 1 Month — best for a first immersion, short academic breaks, or exploratory placement.
  • 4 Months — enough time to integrate, contribute meaningfully, and build real experience.
  • 6 Months — ideal for deeper professional development and long-term cultural integration.

Exact schedules and structure depend on your location and placement type. We’ll confirm details after your application.
