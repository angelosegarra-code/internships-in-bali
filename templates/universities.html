Academic alignment

We support universities seeking reliable internship placements in Indonesia. Programs can be aligned with credit-bearing internships, independent study requirements, or supervised experiential learning.

Program structure & supervision

Participants receive placement matching, a clear program timeline, local coordination, and check-ins during the internship period. Universities may designate a faculty supervisor while we coordinate local support.

Documentation available

  • Program overview and schedule
  • Placement confirmation letter
  • Learning-outcome summary (available on request)
  • Local supervisor or mentor details (where applicable)
  • End-of-program confirmation (participation / dates)

Locations & placement fields

Programs are offered in Bali and, based on field availability, in Lombok, Yogyakarta, and Jakarta. Placement fields may include business and marketing, media and communications, hospitality and tourism, arts and design, NGOs and community projects, education support, and sustainability / social impact.
